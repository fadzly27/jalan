## 1. Install Jalan on your iPhone

Install it once from Safari and it opens full screen like any other app.

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone. It must be Safari, not Chrome.
2. Go to **[fadzly27.github.io/jalan](https://fadzly27.github.io/jalan/)**.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
5. Open **Jalan** from your Home Screen.

After the first launch Jalan works with no internet connection. A sample trip, Seoul – Busan, is already there so you can look around. Remove it later in **Settings → Clear sample data**.

## 2. The Trips screen

This is the first screen you see. From top to bottom:

- **Settings** (gear, top left) and **+** (top right, add a trip).
- **Next trip card**: your nearest trip, with a big "days to go" countdown and three numbers: **Ready** (how prepared you are), **Packed** (items packed) and **Spent** (share of budget used). Tap the card to open the trip.
- **Things to do** (orange row): reminders such as missing documents or check-in opening. Tap it to see them all. Tap one to go there, or **×** to dismiss it.
- **Upcoming / Past**: switch between trips still to come and trips already finished.

If two trips run back to back (for example Manila, then Seoul), the card offers **Link trips?**. Linked trips show as one journey with combined dates.

## 3. Add your first trip

You need at least one country and the dates; everything else is optional.

1. On the Trips screen, tap **+** (top right).
2. **Name**: type a name, for example "Bali holiday". Leave it empty and Jalan uses the city names.
3. Under **Destinations**, tap **Choose country**, search for the country and tap it. Then type the city next to it.
4. Visiting more than one place? Tap **Add destination** and repeat step 3.
5. Set the **Start** and **End** dates. The number of nights shows underneath.
6. Optional: type a **Total** budget in your home currency (RM by default).
7. Optional: tap **Add cover photo** to pick a picture.
8. Tap **Add** (top right).

If something is missing, Jalan tells you what, and **Choose country** turns orange. The local currency (for example ₩ for South Korea) is set from the country automatically.

To change or delete a trip later, open it and tap **Edit** at the top right.

## 4. Inside a trip: the five tabs

Open a trip and use the tab bar at the bottom. **‹ Trips** at the top left takes you back.

| Tab | What it shows | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Countdown, Trip ready %, next plan, to-do list | Tick to-dos, tap a tile to jump to a tab, tap **Edit** to change the trip |
| Itinerary | Your plans day by day | Add, edit, reorder and delete plans; open places in Maps |
| Packing | Packing list and progress | Tick items, add suggestions, save or use templates |
| Docs | Passport, tickets, bookings, insurance, visa | Save photos or PDFs, check warnings, edit the emergency card |
| Budget | Spending against your budget, in two currencies | Add expenses, set budgets, change the exchange rate |

**Trip ready %** is the average of three things: items packed, required documents saved (passport, flight, hotel, insurance) and to-dos done.

## 5. Plan your days (Itinerary)

Each day of the trip has its own timeline, coloured by category: Flight, Transport, Stay, Food, Sightseeing and Free time.

**Add a plan**

1. Tap a day in the date strip at the top.
2. Tap **+**.
3. Type a **Title** (for example "Flight to Seoul") and a **Place**.
4. Pick a **Category**, then set the **Day** and **Time**.
5. Optional: attach a saved document (for example your boarding pass) and add notes.
6. Tap **Add**.

**Change a plan**: tap it, edit, then tap **Save**. **Delete Plan** is at the bottom of the same sheet.

**Reorder plans**: tap **Edit** at the top, then use the up and down arrows. Tap **Done** when finished.

**Get directions**: open a plan that has a place and tap **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps**.

**Country for the day**: the button under the date (for example "South Korea · ₩ KRW") sets which country you are in that day. New expenses on that day use its currency.

## 6. Pack (Packing)

The bar at the top shows how much is packed, for example "10 of 18 packed".

1. **Tick an item**: tap it. It gets a green check and is crossed out. Tap again to untick.
2. **Add your own item**: tap **+**, type the item, pick a category and tap **Add**. Press return to add several in a row.
3. **Add a suggestion**: scroll to **Suggested for … nights** and tap any item. Suggestions follow the trip length and the weather, for example hand warmers for winter.
4. **Filter**: tap **Clothes**, **Electronics**, **Toiletries**, **Documents** or **Others** to see one group.
5. **Delete items**: tap **Edit**, then the red minus next to an item.

**Reuse a list next time**: tap **…** (top right) → **Save as Template**. On your next trip, tap **…** → **Use** next to the template. The same menu has **Untick all items**.

## 7. Save your documents (Docs)

Photos and PDFs you save here stay on your phone and open without internet.

**Add a document**

1. Tap **+**.
2. Pick the type: Passport, Flight ticket, Hotel booking, Travel insurance, Visa / entry or Other.
3. Add a title, the booking or passport number, and the expiry date if it has one.
4. Choose a status: **Valid**, **Booked**, **To check** or **Missing**.
5. Tap **Add photo or PDF** and take a photo or pick a file.
6. Tap **Add**.

Tap any card to view the file, replace it or delete the document.

**Warnings**: orange boxes at the top tell you when travel insurance is missing or your passport expires within 6 months of the trip. Tap a warning to fix it.

**Lock with a PIN**: go to **Settings → Set PIN for Documents** and choose 4 digits. The Docs tab then asks for the PIN. Tap the lock icon to lock it again.

**Emergency card**: at the bottom of Docs, tap **Edit** to add emergency contacts, blood type, medical notes and your embassy. Tap **Share** to send it by message or email.

## 8. Track your spending (Budget)

All totals are counted in your home currency, so you always know what the trip costs you in RM.

**Read the summary**

- The switch at the top (for example **RM | ₩ Won**) changes every amount on the screen to that currency.
- The dark card shows **Spent**, your total budget, money **Left** and how much you can spend **Per day** for the rest of the trip.
- **By category** shows Flights, Stay, Transport, Food, Shopping and Activities. A category over its budget turns orange.
- Tap the pencil on the dark card, or any category, to set the total and category budgets.

**Add an expense**

1. Tap the blue **+** at the top right.
2. Type the amount on the keypad.
3. Check the currency you paid in. It starts on the currency of the country you are in that day.
4. The green pill shows the converted amount, for example "₩ 15,000 → ≈ RM 45.99".
5. Pick a category. Optional: add a note, change the date or attach a receipt photo.
6. Tap **Add Expense**.

Tap any expense under **Recent** to edit or delete it.

**Exchange rate**: the line under the dark card shows the rate in use, for example "RM 1 = ₩ 326 · live rate". Tap **Change** to pick one of three options:

- **Live rate**: downloaded when you are online and saved for offline use.
- **My own rate**: the rate from your money changer receipt. You can also type what you paid and what you got, and Jalan works out the rate.
- **Lock for this trip**: one fixed rate for the whole trip.

The same sheet lets you add another currency for multi-country trips. Every expense keeps the rate used when you added it, so changing the rate never changes past expenses.

## 9. Reminders, backup and settings

**Reminders** appear in the orange "things to do" row each time you open Jalan. They cover:

- 30, 7 and 1 days before a trip
- online check-in, 48 hours before a flight
- items still unpacked the day before you leave
- missing documents or a passport expiring soon
- categories over budget
- a monthly reminder to back up

Jalan has no push notifications, so open the app now and then to see them.

**Back up once a month.** Your data lives only on your phone, so a backup file is your safety net.

1. Tap the gear (top left of Trips) to open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Export all data**. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.
3. To restore, tap **Import backup** and pick that file. It replaces everything on the phone.

**Other settings**

- **Home currency**: the currency all totals use (RM by default).
- **Appearance**: System, Light or Dark.
- **Set PIN for Documents**: lock the Docs tab with 4 digits.
- **Clear sample data**: removes the Seoul – Busan example trip.

## 10. Common questions

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes, after you open it once online. Only the live exchange rate needs internet; the last rate is saved and used offline.

**Where is my data stored? Can anyone else see it?**
Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server, so nobody else can see your trips or documents.

**I got a new phone. How do I move my trips?**
On the old phone, export a backup (section 9). Install Jalan on the new phone, then use **Import backup** with that file.

**The Add button on New Trip does nothing.**
A destination country is missing. Tap **Choose country** under Destinations, pick a country, then tap **Add**.

**How do I get the newest version?**
Open Jalan, close it fully (swipe it away), then open it again. It updates by itself.

**Will deleting the app lose my data?**
Yes. Removing Jalan from the Home Screen, or clearing Safari website data, deletes your trips. Export a backup first.
