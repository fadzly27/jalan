Jalan is a travel planner for group trips, at home and abroad. It keeps the countdown, plans, packing, documents, your budget and the group fund in one place. It works offline, needs no account, and stores everything on your phone only.

## 1. Install Jalan on your iPhone

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone (not Chrome).
2. Go to **[fadzly27.github.io/jalan](https://fadzly27.github.io/jalan/)**.
3. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
5. Open **Jalan** from your Home Screen. It works without internet after the first launch.

## 2. The Trips screen

- **Next trip card**: your nearest trip with a big "days to go", plus **Ready**, **Packed** and **Spent**. Tap it to open the trip.
- **Things to do** (orange row): reminders such as missing documents or people who haven't paid into the fund. Tap to see them all.
- **Link trips?**: shows when two trips run back to back; linked trips show as one journey.
- **Upcoming / Past**: switch between trips still to come and finished ones.
- **Gear** (top left) opens Settings. **+** (top right) adds a trip.

## 3. Add a trip

1. Tap **+** on the Trips screen.
2. Type a **Trip name**.
3. Tap **Choose country**, pick it, then type the city. More stops? Tap **Add destination**.
4. Check **Domestic trip**. It turns on by itself when every stop is in Malaysia, and you can change it. See [Domestic trips](#11-domestic-trips).
5. Set the **Start** and **End** dates and **My budget**.
6. Under **Travellers**, tap **+ Add** and type each name. Names only: nobody gets an invite or needs the app.
7. Collecting money? Turn on **Group fund** and type the amount per person, e.g. "Collect RM 150 each".
8. Tap **Create**.

If a country is missing, Jalan tells you and **Choose country** turns orange. Change or delete a trip later with **Edit**.

## 4. Inside a trip: the five tabs

| Tab | What it shows | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Countdown, Trip ready %, next plan, to-dos | Tick to-dos, tap a tile to jump to a tab, **See all** for the full to-do list |
| Itinerary | Travellers and plans day by day | Add people and plans, assign who handles what, share the plan |
| Packing | Packing list and progress | Tick items, add suggestions, save templates |
| Docs | Tickets, bookings, IC, passport, insurance | Save photos or PDFs, check warnings, emergency card |
| Budget | My spending and the group fund | Add expenses and fund entries, see who owes what |

## 5. Plans and people

**Add people**: on the travellers card, tap **Add people**, type a name and tap **Add**. Each person gets a colour.

**Add a plan**

1. Tap a day, then **+**.
2. Type a title and place, then pick a category: Flight, Transport, **Drive**, Stay, Food, Sightseeing or Free time.
3. For **Drive**, fill in **From**, **To** and about how long. It shows as "Kuantan → Cameron Highlands · about 4 h".
4. Under **Handled by**, pick a person and role (Booked by, Buys tickets, Drives, In charge). It shows as a green label like "Booked by Adam".
5. Set the time and tap **Add**.

Tap a plan for Apple Maps or Google Maps directions. Tap **Edit** to reorder.

## 6. Share with your group

In Itinerary, tap the **share button** next to **+**. Choose **Whole trip** or **This day**, then:

- **Download PDF**: one A4 section per day, with your name and logo if set. Works offline.
- **Send update to WhatsApp**: plain text, day by day. Type what changed first (e.g. "Dinner moved to 8 PM") and it becomes the first line.
- **Add to Calendar**: an .ics file with one event per plan at the right local time.

Shared copies show plans, times and places only. Costs, notes and documents are never included. The group fund summary goes in the PDF only if you switch it on.

## 7. Pack your bag

- Tap an item to tick it. Tap **+** to add your own.
- Scroll down for suggestions based on trip length and destination: jacket for the highlands, swimwear for the beach, winter wear for cold countries.
- **•••** → **Save as Template** to reuse the list next time.

## 8. Keep your documents

1. Tap **+** and pick the type: passport, ticket, hotel booking, insurance, visa, **IC / ID card** or other.
2. Tap **Add photo or PDF**.
3. Set the status (Valid, Booked, To check, Missing) and tap **Add**.

Orange warnings flag missing insurance or a passport expiring within 6 months (abroad only). **Settings → Set PIN for Documents** locks the tab. The **Emergency card** at the bottom can be shared with family.

## 9. Track your own spending

Open **Budget → My spending**.

1. Tap the round **+**.
2. Type the amount. Abroad, the green bubble shows it in RM, e.g. "₩ 15,000 → ≈ RM 45.99".
3. Pick a category and tap **Add Expense**.

The dark card shows **Spent** (including your share of the group fund), **Left** and **Per day**. Abroad, the line under it shows the exchange rate; tap **Change** to use your own rate or lock one.

## 10. Run the group fund

One person keeps the shared money and records it in **Budget → Group fund**. Tap **+** and choose:

- **Money in**: someone pays into the fund (cash or transfer).
- **Paid from fund**: a shared cost paid with fund money, split between everyone or chosen people.
- **Someone paid**: a member paid for the group with their own money. The fund now owes them.

**Cashflow** lists every entry with the balance after it. **Members** shows each person's **Paid** and **Share**, and whether they **Get back**, **Owe** or are **Settled**. People below the per-person amount show "still to pay" and appear in your reminders.

At the end, **Settle up** lists the fewest transfers needed. Tap **Mark as settled** when done. **Share summary to WhatsApp** sends the balance, everyone's result and the last 5 entries.

## 11. Domestic trips

| | Domestic | Abroad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money | All in RM, no exchange rates | RM and local currency, with rates |
| Budget | Adds Petrol, Toll, Parking | Flights, Stay, Food and more |
| Documents | Bookings, tickets, IC | Also passport, visa, insurance checks |
| To-do ideas | Service the car, top up Touch 'n Go | eSIM, currency, visa rules |
| Reminders | Car and Touch 'n Go a week before | Online check-in 48 hours before flights |
| Packing | By destination (highlands, beach) | By season of the country |

## 12. Your profile and backups

**Settings → Organiser profile**: add your name or business, phone, email and logo. They appear on PDFs and at the end of WhatsApp messages.

**Back up once a month**: **Settings → Export all data** saves one file with trips, travellers, the group fund and documents. On a new phone, use **Import backup** with that file. Deleting the app or clearing Safari website data deletes your trips.

## 13. Common questions

**Do my travellers need the app?**
No. They are just names. Send them the plan as a PDF or WhatsApp message.

**Can my group see the costs?**
Only what you choose to send. Shared plans never include costs or documents.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes, after opening it once, including PDFs. Only the live exchange rate needs internet.

**Some place names are missing in the PDF.**
The PDF only shows Latin letters, so names in Korean, Japanese, Chinese or Thai are left out there. WhatsApp text and calendar events keep them.

**How do I get the newest version?**
Open Jalan, close it fully, then open it again.
