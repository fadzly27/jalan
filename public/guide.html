How to use Jalan

Your trip countdown, plans, packing list, documents and budget, all in one app. This guide takes about 5 minutes.

Open Jalan

The short version

  1. Install it once from Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Add a trip with . Don't forget to tap Choose country.
  3. Back up once a month in Settings. Your trips live only on your phone.

1

Install on your iPhone

You only do this once. Afterwards Jalan opens from your Home Screen like any app.

Open this page in Safari and tap Open Jalan at the top.It must be Safari. Chrome on iPhone can't install apps like this.

Tap the Share buttonThe square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.

Tap Add to Home ScreenCan't see it? Scroll down a little in that menu.

Tap AddThe blue Jalan icon appears on your Home Screen.

Always open Jalan from its Home Screen icon. It works without internet after the first time.

2

The Trips screen

This is the first thing you see. Your next trip is always at the top.

What the numbers mean

Days to go
How many days until the trip starts.
Ready
How prepared you are: packing, documents and to-dos together.
Packed
Items ticked off your packing list.
Spent
How much of your budget you have used.
Orange row
Things to do, like missing documents. Tap it to see them.

The gear at the top left opens Settings. The at the top right adds a trip.

3

Add a trip

You only need a country and the dates. Everything else is optional.

Tap at the top right of Trips

Type a NameFor example "Japan spring trip". Optional.

Tap Choose country, search, and tap the countryThen type the city next to it. Going to more places? Tap Add destination.

Pick the Start and End datesJalan counts the nights for you.

Type your total Budget in RMOptional. You can add a cover photo too.

Tap Add at the top right

Most common mistake: typing a city without tapping Choose country. If you forget, Jalan tells you and the button turns orange.

5

Plan your days

Open the Itinerary tab. Each day has its own list of plans.

Tap a day in the strip at the top

Tap , type what and whereFor example "Flight to Seoul" at "KL International Airport".

Pick a category and a time, then tap AddFlight, Transport, Stay, Food, Sightseeing or Free time. Each has its own colour.

Need directions? Tap a plan, then Apple Maps or Google Maps

Change the order: tap Edit and use the arrows

6

Pack your bag

Open the Packing tab. Tick things off as they go in the bag.

Tap an item to tick itIt turns green and gets crossed out. Tap again to undo.

Add your own with

Scroll down for suggestions and tap to addBased on how long you're away and the weather, like hand warmers in winter.

Reuse your list next trip: ••• then Save as Template

7

Keep your documents

Open the Docs tab. Save photos of your passport, tickets and bookings so you have them even without internet.

Tap and pick the typePassport, flight ticket, hotel booking, insurance, visa or other.

Tap Add photo or PDFTake a photo, or choose a screenshot or PDF.

Set the status and tap AddGreen = Valid or Booked. Orange = To check or Missing.

Orange boxes at the top warn you, for example when insurance is missing or your passport expires within 6 months of the trip. Tap one to fix it.

Want privacy? Settings → Set PIN for Documents locks this tab with 4 digits.

At the bottom is your Emergency card: contacts, blood type and embassy. Tap Share to send it to family.

8

Track your money

Open the Budget tab. Jalan works in two currencies at once, so you always know the price in RM.

Add what you spend

Tap the blue at the top right

Type the amount on the keypadThe green bubble shows it in RM straight away, like "₩ 15,000 → RM 45.99".

Check the currency you paid inIt picks the local currency for you. Paid in RM? Tap RM.

Pick a category and tap Add ExpenseOptional: a note, another date, or a photo of the receipt.

Got a better rate at the money changer? Tap Change next to the exchange rate and choose My own rate. Old expenses keep the rate they were saved with.

9

Back up once a month

Your trips are saved only on your phone. Nobody else can see them, so a backup file is your safety net.

On the Trips screen, tap the gear to open Settings

Tap Export all dataSave the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself.

To restore on a new phone: Import backup and pick that file

Deleting the Jalan icon, or clearing Safari's website data, also deletes your trips. Back up first.

Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After you open it once, everything works offline. Only the live exchange rate needs internet, and the last rate is saved.

The Add button does nothing

A country is missing. Tap Choose country under Destinations, pick one, then tap Add.

How do I get the newest version?

Open Jalan, close it fully (swipe it away), then open it again. It updates by itself.

How do I remove the sample trip?

Go to Settings → Clear sample data. It removes the Seoul – Busan example.

Can I change or delete a trip?

Open the trip and tap Edit at the top right. Delete Trip is at the bottom.

Does Jalan send me notifications?

No. Reminders show in the orange "things to do" row when you open the app, so open it now and then.
